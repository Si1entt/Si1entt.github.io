abstract: >-
  Grounded in Moral Foundations Theory, we apply cross-lingual linear probing to reveal a shared yet culturally nuanced English–Chinese moral subspace inside LLMs and extract causally steerable Moral Vectors. We further propose Adaptive Moral Fusion (AMF), a lightweight dynamic-gating activation intervention that substantially reduces HarmBench jailbreak rates and XSTest false refusals while preserving general model utility.
