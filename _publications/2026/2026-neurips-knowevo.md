abstract: >-
  We reframe LLM scientific adaptation from parameter fine-tuning to explicitly evolving external, modular expertise blocks organized into strategy mutation trees via multi-agent forward exploration and backward revision. Experiments on protein stability and solubility optimization demonstrate faster convergence, higher sample efficiency, and superior design quality over reinforcement learning baselines.
