abstract: >-
  We introduce FocalLaw, the first full-process Chinese civil judicial dataset centered on the judge-summarized dispute focus (1,000 cases filtered from 730,000 judgment–trial video pairs across six causes of action), and uncover the counter-intuitive "Clerk Trap" where LLMs summarize claims rather than adjudicate facts even under CoT and SFT. To bridge this gap, we propose FocalJudge, a structured judge-like cognitive workflow that effectively leverages dispute focuses to boost legal judgment prediction and fact-finding.
