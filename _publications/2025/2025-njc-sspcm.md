abstract: >-
  We investigate the synergistic molecular structural factors—anion radius, alkyl chain length, and chain number—governing solid–solid phase transitions in quaternary ammonium salts for thermal energy storage. Using Avalon molecular fingerprints across SVM, Random Forest, and DNN classifiers, our optimal model achieves 95.24% accuracy (PR-AUC 0.97) in screening candidate solid–solid phase change materials.
